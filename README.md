# Fundamentals of Radar-based Microwave Imaging

Interactive slides by Dr Adnan Elahi (University of Galway) for the Training on Radar-based Imaging Algorithms, 3BAtwin project (Horizon Europe grant agreement No. 101159623).

Online version: https://adnanelahi.github.io/radar-imaging-fundamentals/

The slides derive the delay-and-sum (DAS) beamformer from the signal model of Curtis, Lavoie and Fear (2017), and cover weighting factors, MIST, Capon and robust Capon beamforming, and early-time artefact removal. All signals in the interactive figures are computed in the browser from the stated models. Figures reproduced from published work are credited on the slide where they appear.
